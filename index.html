The chain · 5 blocks

Each block does one job. The number only shows up when the whole chain runs.

Most clinics buy a single block — someone to post reels — and then wonder why nothing changed at the front desk. The gap is never the posting. Here is the whole chain, in the order it runs.

01
What to say

15–20 scripts a month, and every one answers a question your patients actually ask — we pull those from your reviews, your competitors’ reviews and the forums before a word is written. 02
Filmed in minutes

Filming — 20 minutes a week, and a shoot day a month

Two lanes, and they run together: short pieces you film on a phone, with instructions down to which tripod to buy and where to stand — and once a month I come in and shoot a batch day, twelve reels per trip, with the light and the framing a phone won’t give you.

After you tap record, nothing leaves your hands. We edit and we post. The take, the finished edit and the published reel sit in one place and you watch them there — no files to send over, no second app to install, no thread to dig through. You film, and you film to a script written for your niche, not to a generic content calendar. The weekly phone lane is in both ways of running it; the shoot day belongs to the second one.
