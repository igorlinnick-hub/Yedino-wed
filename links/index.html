Questions clinics ask

Straight answers.

What exactly do you build?

A patient-acquisition system: a content system for Instagram, automated patient DMs, a landing page per treatment, and a CRM pipeline with follow-up. We build it and run it — you don't need an in-house marketer.

Will this work for my type of clinic?

We work only with wellness and regenerative clinics — ketamine and mental health, IV & NAD+, stem cell and joint regeneration, functional medicine, medical weight loss. If your patients research treatments before booking, the system applies.

Do we need to run ads?

No. The system is built on organic Instagram interest first. If you already run ads or want to, we align the funnel so paid traffic lands in the same automation — but it's not a requirement.

How much work is this for our staff?

About an hour a week at the start — treatment details, a few filming slots, approvals. After that the system runs; your front desk only sees booked consultations in the calendar.

How fast does it go live?

DM automation and the first treatment page are typically live within the first weeks; the full system, including the content engine, within the first month. Results compound from there.

What happens on the growth audit call?

Twenty minutes. We look at your Instagram, website and current follow-up together and map where patients are being lost. You keep the map whether or not we work together.
